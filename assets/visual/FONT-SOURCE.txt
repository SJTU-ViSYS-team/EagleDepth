Paper Computer Modern

The comparison SVG files embed PaperComputerModern.ttf, a Unicode-mapped
derivative of BaKoMa Computer Modern Roman 10 (cmr10.ttf), distributed with
Matplotlib. Original Computer Modern outlines are preserved. Dagger, double
dagger and symbol punctuation come from BaKoMa cmsy10.ttf; font-specific
TrueType hints are removed from merged glyphs. The derivative is renamed
Paper Computer Modern.

Original fonts: Basil K. Malyshev
http://www.ctan.org/tex-archive/fonts/cm/ps-type1/bakoma
